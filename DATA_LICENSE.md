# Data terms

The code is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The data in `data/`, and the files
`make data` downloads, are not: they keep the terms of their providers.

## SAMeT (CPTEC/INPE)

The CPTEC SAMeT product data in this repository are for restricted use and may not be sold. Use of the data is subject to the following conditions:

- Commercial use of the CPTEC SAMeT product data is not permitted.
- The data may not be resold or redistributed commercially.

For any questions about data use, please contact CPTEC/INPE at atende.cptec@inpe.br.

The [SAMeT read-me](https://ftp.cptec.inpe.br/modelos/tempo/SAMeT/Read-me.pdf) describes the product but states no use terms of its own, so
ask CPTEC/INPE before any use beyond these. The monthly climatologies cover 2000–2023,
according to the [climatology read-me](https://ftp.cptec.inpe.br/modelos/tempo/SAMeT/CLIMATOLOGY/READ-ME.txt).

## State boundaries (IBGE)

`data/BR_UF_2022.zip` is the 2022 federative-unit boundary layer of the Instituto
Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE), *Malha Territorial 2022*
(https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/15774-malhas.html),
published as open public data; cite IBGE as the source.

## Natural Earth

Maps made without `--shapefile` draw coastlines and borders from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
